# Where we differ from the paper, and why

For each difference between our result and the paper's, rank the explanations:
1. A stated difference (different version, split, or library; we know it).
2. An unstated choice the paper left open, where our choice plausibly moves the number (point to the D-row).
3. An error, theirs or ours. Claim this only with evidence, and check ours first.
Include: which graphs were misclassified, their score against the threshold, seed spread and margin, and what experiment would settle it.
A documented negative result earns full marks on this criterion.
